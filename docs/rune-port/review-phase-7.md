named in the table (debug build, this Mac). Paths are under `rune/crates/mdaw-app/` unless noted.

  every pair inspected. Remaining differences: [phase-7-beat-ui.md § Screenshots](phase-7-beat-ui.md#screenshots-docsparityphase-7).
